Put your real university logo file here and rename it to:

    university-logo.png

Recommended: square or near-square PNG, transparent background,
at least 200x200px. The header shows it at 40x40px and the footer
at 40x40px, so it will be scaled down — it does not need to be huge.

Until you add the real file, the page will simply hide the broken
image icon (this is handled by the onerror="this.style.display='none'"
attribute on the <img> tags in index.html), so the layout still looks
fine without it.
