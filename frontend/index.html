One platform, three connected parts

UniMate AI is not a static university website. It is a working portal where public information, a student's private academic record, and a generative AI layer read from the same data.

University Portal

Departments, programs, faculty, admissions, campus information and contact details — open to every visitor.

View academics

Student Portal

Courses, timetable, assignments, attendance, grades and GPA — a private dashboard for each registered student.

Go to student login

Generative AI Layer

An AI assistant that reads a student's own courses, attendance and goals, then gives advice built for that student.
